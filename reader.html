What it will do

  • Reads what you havePDFs, EPUBs, web articles, and any text you select in any app.
  • Follow alongEach sentence is highlighted as it is read. Change the speed, skip back a sentence.
  • Take it with youSave any document as an audio file for a walk or a commute. On a MacBook Air M2 the voice makes audio about 11 times faster than it plays, so a 30-page paper takes under 10 minutes.
  • Private by designNo account, no cloud, no tracking inside the app. Your documents never leave your Mac.
  • Yours to keepOne price. No subscription, no word limits, no trial that turns into a charge.

Questions

Which Macs?

Macs with Apple silicon, on macOS 26.2 or later.

How is this different from the Mac's built-in Speak Selection?

Reader is made for whole documents: a PDF read from start to finish without selecting anything, headings and page clutter handled, a sentence you can follow, and audio files you can keep. Listen to the sample and judge the voice yourself.

Who makes it?

One independent developer, the maker of Rubidu. Reader uses Rubidu's on-device voice.
